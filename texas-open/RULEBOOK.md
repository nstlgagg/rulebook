# NSTLGA Texas Open Rulebook

The Texas Open is a three-day LAN for 8 teams at Tech Port Esports in San Antonio, Oct 30 – Nov 1, 2026: two GSL groups of 4, then a 4-team single-elimination playoff. Every series is Bo3 except the Bo5 grand final.

| Time zone | Central Time (CT) |

**Six rules that decide matches**

1. **Default time is final.** You can never play later than the default time. Earlier only if both teams agree and a referee confirms.
2. **Veto opens early.** The map veto starts 5 minutes before the default time. A 4:00 PM match starts its veto at 3:55 PM.
3. **20 minutes to be ready.** All five players seated, set up and ready within 20 minutes of the default time, or it is a forfeit.
4. **Wait for the referee.** A match is not live until a referee confirms, and again after any restart. Early starts do not count.
5. **Be set up before you sit.** Config, peripherals and settings done in your own time. A late setup is not an excuse.
6. **Your own account.** No smurfing, no account sharing, no stand-ins that were not approved. At least 3 of your 5 registered players play every match.

## 1 The Event

The NSTLGA Texas Open is Stop III of IV on the 2026 NA CS2 Circuit, played on site at Tech Port Esports in San Antonio. By taking part you agree to these rules. Admins have the final say, and anything not covered is ruled on in the spirit of fair competition. Rule changes are published as a new version and announced in the NSTLGA Discord. NSTLGA runs the event with reference to Valve's [Tournament Operating Requirements](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_rules_and_regs/blob/main/tournament-operation-requirements.md) and seeds from Valve's official monthly VRS ranking (Section 3).

**Group stage: 2 groups of 4, GSL double elimination**

All group series are Bo3. Two wins put a team into the top 2 of its group. Two losses send it home. Every group finishes by the 1-1 deciders on Saturday at 1:00 PM.

| Series | Who plays | Winner | Loser |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opener 1 | Group #1 v #4 | Winners match | Elimination match |
| Opener 2 | Group #2 v #3 | Winners match | Elimination match |
| Winners match (1-0) | Both opener winners | 1st, advances | Decider |
| Elimination match (0-1) | Both opener losers | Decider | 4th, out |
| Decider (1-1) | Winners match loser v Elimination match winner | 2nd, advances | 3rd, out |

**Playoffs: top 4, single elimination**

The top 2 from each group advance. A1 means the group winner of Group A. Each semifinal has its own timeslot on Saturday.

| Match | Teams | Series | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semifinal 1 | A1 v B2 | Bo3 | Sat 4:00 PM |
| Semifinal 2 | B1 v A2 | Bo3 | Sat 7:00 PM |
| 3rd/4th decider | Semifinal losers | Bo3 | Sun 10:00 AM |
| Grand final | Semifinal winners | Bo5 | Sun 3:00 PM |

Teams from the same group cannot meet in the semifinals.

**Prizes**

The live pool is $7,500. Every team that does not advance from its group (3rd and 4th in each group, four teams) takes the same 5th–8th prize.

| Place | Share | Prize | Circuit points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 4th | 5% | $375 | 9 |

- Prizing goes to one team representative, who pays the roster. Payout paperwork comes first. Teams disqualified for cheating forfeit all prizing.
- The prize pool and shares follow the pool NSTLGA publishes; if the pool changes, every prize changes by the same percentage.

## 2 Signing Up & Rosters

- Registration is first come, first served and closed on Oct 20, 2026. Your spot is confirmed once your Team Pass payment clears and you appear on the Public Registry. The Team Pass is $800 per team and non-refundable. If a registered team withdraws, admins fill the spot at their discretion.
- **Players:** a FACEIT account in good standing, a Steam account with no VAC or Game Bans, and age 16 or older (under 18 needs parent or guardian consent on file before Friday media & setup). Anyone previously banned by NSTLGA is ineligible.
- **Rosters:** 5 to 7 players plus one optional coach. At least **3 of your registered players** must play every match. Subs are unlimited if that 3-of-5 core stays.
- **Changes:** roster changes and stand-ins need admin approval **before** the player competes. Ask in a ticket before the event, or at the admin desk on site. An unapproved player is a forfeit. Rosters lock when Friday media & setup begins (10:00 AM).
- **On site:** every player and coach shows a government photo ID at check-in. The name on the ID must match the registered player.

> **PLAY ON YOUR OWN ACCOUNT** Account sharing, smurfing, and ringing mean disqualification and a permanent ban.

## 3 Seeding

Teams with a rank on Valve's official **VRS** (the Oct 5, 2026 monthly list, Americas table) are seeded above all unranked teams, in order of VRS points. Everyone else is ranked by tier, then by points inside the tier. All 8 teams are then snake-drawn into Groups A and B.

**Step 1: tier order for unranked teams**

HLTV Confirmed, Admin Override, ECL, Advanced, Main, Intermediate, Open, then No ESEA / Unverified.

**Step 2: seeding points inside a tier**

| Category | Max | What it measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESEA division | 50 | Your most recent division. ECL playoffs 50, ECL 40, Advanced playoffs 35, Advanced or ESEA Finals 30, Main playoffs 25, Main 20, IM playoffs 15, IM 10, Open 5. |
| HLTV & Liquipedia | 35 | HLTV-covered matches and results at listed events. |
| FACEIT elo | 15 | Average elo of the five players: 3000+ is 15, 2750 is 12, 2500 is 9, 2250 is 6, 2000 is 3, under 2000 is 1. |

Ties break on ESEA points, then elo, then earlier registration.

**Step 3: snake draw**

| Group | Overall seeds |
| --- | --- |
| Group A | 1, 4, 5, 8 |
| Group B | 2, 3, 6, 7 |

Inside a group, teams are numbered #1 to #4 by overall seed, and the opening series are #1 v #4 and #2 v #3.

- **Admin override:** a new team of proven players (division history, elo, FPL, LAN) may be seeded above any non-VRS team. Request it at registration with Steam and FACEIT links.
- **Not final until posted:** seeds are provisional while admins verify rosters, tiers and points. The final seeding and groups are posted in the NSTLGA Discord before Friday media & setup. Seeding decisions are final.

## 4 Scheduling & Check-in

> **THE SHORT VERSION** Every match has a **default time** (Schedule). You **cannot play later** than it. You may only play **earlier**, and **only if both teams agree**.

- Admins set the schedule. There is no self-scheduling to a later slot, and nobody can be forced to play early. If the other team does not agree, the default time stands.
- To play earlier, both teams must have finished their previous series and agree to it at the admin desk. A referee confirms the early start.
- **Check-in:** Friday is media & setup at 10:00 AM. Saturday and Sunday check-in is 9:30 AM. Check in at the admin desk with your full roster and IDs. A team missing at check-in may be forfeited from its first series of the day.
- **Waiting teams:** only 2 series run at once. Teams not on stage may use open stations to play any game they like for warmup. Be back at the player area 30 minutes before your default time.
- Can't make the time? The 20-minute forfeit rule in Section 5 applies.

## 5 Match Day

**Veto, going live, and being ready**

- **Veto** opens **5 minutes before** the default time at the match station, with a referee present. The higher overall seed decides who vetoes first. If a team has not acted within 30 seconds, the referee acts for it and it stands.
- **Bo3 veto:** ban, ban, pick, pick, ban, ban, then the last map is the decider. The team that did not pick a map chooses its starting side. The decider side is decided by a knife round.
- **Bo5 grand final:** ban, ban, then four alternating picks, and the last map is the decider. Sides follow the same rule.
- **Ready deadline:** if your team is not fully seated and ready 20 minutes after the default time, you forfeit. If both miss it, it is a double forfeit. A forfeit is a 13–0 loss on every map not played. We recommend being at your station 15 minutes early.

> **KEY RULE: REFEREE CONFIRMATION** A match is **not live until a referee confirms it**, at the start and **after any restart**. When a map is restarted, wait for the referee to verify it is good to start. **Teams that start without confirmation, or without confirmation after a restart, will not have their round(s) results acknowledged.**

**Server settings**

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Mode & maps | Competitive 5v5 on NSTLGA LAN servers. Map pool is the **current Active Duty map pool** as posted in the NSTLGA Discord; every map is in play for every veto. |
| Start | A referee starts the series once both teams confirm they are ready. |
| Rounds | MR12 (first to 13), sides swap after round 12. Round timer 1:55, bomb 40 seconds. |
| Buy time | 20 seconds |
| Money | $800 start, $16,000 max |
| Overtime | MR3, played to a decisive result. Starting money **$12,500**. |
| Tactical timeouts | 3 per team, 30 seconds each (plus 1 per team in every overtime) |
| Technical pauses | 10 minutes per team, per map |
| Demos | The server records every map. NSTLGA keeps the demos. |

**Pauses**

- Pause at the end of a round, or immediately for a real failure (crash, disconnect, hardware). Do not unpause on your own while a referee is working. Play resumes when both teams confirm or a referee directs it.
- Going over your 10 technical minutes without approval can cost the map. Stalling or using pauses to disrupt momentum is punishable.

## 6 Stations, Equipment & the LAN Floor

> **THE KEY POINT** An unprepared station is treated like a player who did not show up. If setup pushes you past the 20-minute limit, you forfeit with no exception.

**House stations**

- Every station runs a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, Radeon RX 9070 XT, 32 GB DDR5, a ZOWIE 400 Hz monitor and a Vertagear chair. All stations are identical. You cannot request a different one.
- Stations are assigned by the referees. Teams may use stations for warmup and practice only when no series needs them.

**Your own equipment**

- Bring your own mouse, keyboard, mousepad and headset. Peripherals are inspected by a referee at setup and may be inspected again at any time. Wired headsets only on stage.
- No macros, remapping software or extra drivers beyond what a referee approves. Configs are loaded from a USB drive checked by a referee, or typed in on the station.
- Phones, smart watches and any other connected devices stay away from the stage. Recording, streaming and messaging from the stage are not allowed.
- Use Friday media & setup to test your config, mic, sensitivity and monitor settings. Late setup is not an excuse for a delay.

**If something breaks mid-match**

- Genuine failures (crash, disconnect, hardware, server) allow a technical pause. Preparation failures (no config loaded, peripheral not checked in, late arrival) are not protected.
- If a player cannot return in time, play on with the rest or forfeit the map.
- Round restores happen only if the server demonstrably failed, and play resumes only after referee confirmation.

**Supervision**

- Referees watch the stage and may inspect a player's station, peripherals or screen at any time. Refusing or failing a check means a map or match forfeit, possible disqualification, or a ban.

## 7 Coaches

- One coach per team, registered and approved before the first match. An unregistered coach may not take part in any way. A coach may sub in during a series in an emergency.
- **On stage:** the coach sits at the team's coaching position and wears a referee-approved headset, which a referee can monitor at any time. If a referee cannot monitor the coach, the team plays without one.
- **Coach may speak to the team only during** tactical timeouts, halftime, warmup and veto, between maps, and official tech pauses (with referee permission).
- **Never** during live rounds, in any form. Leaving the coaching position loses coaching for the match. Working around supervision (unregistered coach, relay, third party, messages from the audience) is cheating: the team is disqualified and everyone involved is banned.

## 8 Competitive Conduct

- **Cheating:** cheats, scripts, exploits, bug abuse, pixel walking, boosting into unintended spots, ghosting, and stream sniping mean disqualification and a permanent ban from all NSTLGA events.
- **Match fixing and betting:** throwing or arranging results means a permanent ban and lost prizing. Players, coaches, and staff may not bet on any Texas Open match.
- **Behavior:** no harassment, hate speech, threats, or slurs anywhere (Discord, in game, on the floor, toward opponents, admins, staff or spectators). False statements or fake evidence in a protest are a serious offense. Teams answer for their players, coach, and staff.
- **Venue:** follow Tech Port Esports house rules. Damage to equipment or the venue is the responsibility of the team. Admins can remove anyone from the floor.
- **Demos:** NSTLGA keeps the server demos. Teams must hand over any other evidence they hold if asked. Failing to produce requested evidence can be ruled against you.

## 9 Penalties

Admins apply penalties, and they escalate with repeat offenses. Disqualifications are only for offenses in this rulebook and apply to every team equally.

| Offense | Penalty |
| --- | --- |
| Not ready 20 minutes after default time, or station setup unfinished | Forfeit |
| Starting without referee confirmation, or after a restart without it | Round results not acknowledged |
| Unapproved stand-in or ineligible player | Match forfeit; possible DQ |
| Failed or refused equipment or station check | Map or match forfeit; possible DQ and ban |
| Coach left position / coach communicated in live rounds | Coaching lost for the match / match forfeit, possible DQ |
| Pause abuse | Warning, then map forfeit |
| Toxicity or harassment | Warning, suspension, or ban |
| Cheating, tampering with equipment, or match fixing | Permanent ban; no prizing |

- Repeated forfeits can mean removal with no Team Pass refund. Admins decide how a removed team's results are handled, up to voiding them.

## 10 Disputes, Streaming & Final Notes

- **Disputes:** raise a problem with the referee at the time, before the next round starts. For a formal protest, open a ticket in the NSTLGA Discord within 24 hours of the match ending (or of the default time for a no-show). Include both team names, the match, what happened, and evidence (screenshots, demos, photos, timestamps). Do not DM admins. If it is urgent, open the ticket and tag Arr0w or a Head Admin inside it. Admins cannot rule on a forfeit, no-show, or technical failure without a ticket. Head Admin decisions are final.
- **Streaming:** NSTLGA holds the broadcast rights. Matches are broadcast at [nstlga.gg/live](https://nstlga.gg/live) and on Twitch at twitch.tv/nstlga\_gg. Players may not stream from the stage. Taking part lets NSTLGA use your team name, logo, handles, and match footage to promote the event, and you may be asked to join a production voice channel or media session.
- **Spooky Show:** the Sunday Bo1 show match is an exhibition. Participants, format, and prizing are announced by NSTLGA, and it does not count toward the standings.
- **Spectators:** spectators buy a 1-day pass ($25, any one day) or a 3-day pass ($60). Spectators may not communicate with players or coaches during live rounds.
- **Contact:** contact@nstlga.gg, [Discord](https://discord.gg/myVtB58C35). Full circuit rules: nstlga.gg/legal/circuit.
