# NSTLGA Rulebooks

Rulebooks for NSTLGA events ([nstlga.gg](https://www.nstlga.gg)).

- [`texas-open/RULEBOOK.md`](texas-open/RULEBOOK.md): NSTLGA Texas Open (Fire Opal, Stop III of IV, 2026 NA CS2 Circuit), Oct 30 – Nov 1, 2026, Tech Port Esports, San Antonio.
- [`reference/`](reference): Emerald Cup rulebook the Texas Open rules were modeled on (Markdown and original .docx).

Events follow Valve's [Tournament Operating Requirements](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_rules_and_regs/blob/main/tournament-operation-requirements.md) and seed from the official monthly VRS ranking.
