**1 The Event**

The NSTLGA Emerald Cup is a three-day online tournament for 64 teams, played on FACEIT. By taking part you agree to these rules. Admins have the final say, and anything not covered is ruled on in the spirit of fair competition. Rule changes are published as a new version and announced in the NSTLGA Discord.

**Region:** United States & Canada. **Prize pool:** to be announced before registration opens. **Seeding VRS:** Valve’s official monthly VRS ranking (Section 3).

The full schedule is on page 1. Check-in opens 4:00 PM (Pacific) Friday and 9:30 AM Saturday and Sunday.

**Group stage: 8 groups of 8, double elimination**

Two wins put a team into the playoffs. Two losses send it home. Every group finishes after round 3.

| **Round**   | **Series (per group)**    | **Who plays** | **Winners**       | **Losers**       |
|-------------|---------------------------|---------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Round 1** | 4 upper quarterfinals     | All 8 teams   | Upper semis       | Lower round 1    |
| **Round 2** | 2 upper semifinals        | Teams on 1–0  | Upper final       | Lower qualifiers |
| **Round 2** | 2 lower round 1           | Teams on 0–1  | Lower qualifiers  | Out              |
| **Round 3** | Upper final (seeding)     | Teams on 2–0  | Seed 1            | Seed 2           |
| **Round 3** | 2 lower qualifiers (A, B) | Teams on 1–1  | Seed 3 (A), 4 (B) | Out              |

**Playoffs: top 32, single elimination**

The top 4 from each group advance. A1 means the first seed from Group A.

|             | **Quarter 1** | **Quarter 2** | **Quarter 3** | **Quarter 4** |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Match 1** | A1 v B4       | B1 v A4       | C1 v D4       | D1 v C4       |
| **Match 2** | C2 v D3       | D2 v C3       | A2 v B3       | B2 v A3       |
| **Match 3** | E1 v F4       | F1 v E4       | G1 v H4       | H1 v G4       |
| **Match 4** | G2 v H3       | H2 v G3       | E2 v F3       | F2 v E3       |

- Each quarter plays down to one semifinalist: matches 1 v 2 and 3 v 4 meet in the round of 16, and those winners meet in the quarterfinal.

- Semifinals are Quarter 1 v 2 and Quarter 3 v 4. Teams from one group can’t meet before the semifinals.

- Prizing goes to one team representative, who pays the roster. Payout paperwork comes first. Teams disqualified for cheating forfeit all prizing.

**2 Signing Up & Rosters**

- Registration is first come, first served. Times are posted publicly in the NSTLGA Discord. Any Team Pass is non-refundable, and your spot is confirmed once payment clears and you appear on the Public Registry.

- **Players:** a FACEIT account in good standing, a Steam account with no VAC or Game Bans, and age 16 or older (under 18 needs parent or guardian consent). Anyone previously banned by NSTLGA is ineligible.

- **Rosters:** 5 to 7 players plus one optional coach. At least **3 of your registered players** must play every match. Subs are unlimited if that 3-of-5 core stays.

- **Changes:** roster changes and stand-ins need admin approval by ticket **before** the player competes. An unapproved player is a forfeit. Rosters lock when Friday check-in opens.

**3 Seeding**

Teams with a rank on Valve’s official **VRS** (the latest monthly list before registration closes) are seeded above all unranked teams. Everyone else is ranked by a 100-point score, then all 64 are snake-drawn into Groups A–H.

| **Category**          | **Max** | **What it measures**                                                                                                                          |
|-----------------------|---------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **ESEA division**     | 50      | Your most recent division. ECL 40, Advanced 30, Main 20, IM 10, Open 5. Reaching that league’s playoffs adds 5; ESEA Finals attendance is 30. |
| **HLTV & Liquipedia** | 25      | VRS rank, HLTV-covered matches, and results at listed online events.                                                                          |
| **FACEIT elo**        | 15      | Individual player elo. FPL and Challenger players score highly.                                                                               |
| **LAN**               | 7       | Named offline events in the last year. Placements count above attendance.                                                                     |
| **Other**             | 3       | Any other verifiable result.                                                                                                                  |

- **Snake draw:** seeds 1–8 go to Groups A–H in order, seeds 9–16 go back H–A, and the direction keeps alternating until all 64 teams are placed.

- **Admin override:** a new team of proven players (division history, elo, FPL, LAN) may be seeded above any non-VRS team. Request it at registration with Steam and FACEIT links. Seeding decisions are final.

**5 Match Day**

**Veto, going live, and being ready**

- **Pick/ban** opens **5 minutes before** the default time. Be in the matchroom. If the FACEIT timer expires, the platform picks and it stands. The higher seed has veto advantage.

- **Ready deadline:** if your team isn’t fully readied (all five connected and **.ready** typed) 20 minutes after the default time, you forfeit. If both miss it, it’s a double forfeit. A forfeit is a 13–0 loss on every map not played. We recommend being ready 15 minutes early.

**Pauses**

- Pause at the end of a round, or immediately for a real failure (crash, disconnect, hardware). Don’t unpause on your own while an admin is working. Play resumes when both teams confirm or an admin directs it.

- Going over your 10 technical minutes without approval can cost the map. Stalling or using pauses to disrupt momentum is punishable.

**FACEIT Anti-Cheat**

- Required for every player in every match. If it won’t run, you can’t play.

- It needs a full restart after installing or updating. Sort out Secure Boot, TPM, overlay, and virtualization blockers ahead of time.

- Tampering with or circumventing it means disqualification and a permanent ban.

**An hour before your match**

- Run Windows Update and reboot. Launch CS and let it patch. Update Steam and FACEIT AC.

- Test headset, mic, webcam, and internet. Check your config in an offline server.

**If something breaks mid-match**

- Genuine failures (crash, disconnect, hardware, server) allow a technical pause. Preparation failures (pending update, no anti-cheat, never restarted) are not protected.

- If a player can’t return in time, play on with the rest or forfeit the map.

- Round restores happen only if the server demonstrably failed, and play resumes only after admin confirmation.

**Webcams**

- Every player needs a working webcam or phone camera. Having one is a condition of entry.

- Checks are random and unannounced, in any match. Selected quarterfinal, semifinal, and grand final matches require every player on cam for the whole match.

- The feed must be live and unobstructed, show most of your setup and screens, and stay in frame. Admins may ask for a screen share, room scan, or peripherals check.

- Refusing or failing a check means a map or match forfeit, possible disqualification, or a ban.

**7 Coaches**

- One coach per team, registered and approved before the first match. An unregistered coach may not take part in any way. A coach may sub in during a series in an emergency.

- **Verified voice channel:** the coach joins the NSTLGA verified voice channel with an admin before the match and stays for its full length. The admin can hear them and may ask for a screen share. If no admin is available, the team plays without a coach.

- **Coach may speak to the team only during** tactical timeouts, halftime, warmup and veto, between maps, and official tech pauses (with admin permission).

- **Never** during live rounds, in any form. Leaving the channel loses coaching for the match. Working around supervision (unregistered coach, relay, third party) is cheating: the team is disqualified and everyone involved is banned.

**8 Competitive Conduct**

- **Cheating:** cheats, scripts, exploits, bug abuse, pixel walking, boosting into unintended spots, ghosting, and stream sniping mean disqualification and a permanent ban from all NSTLGA events.

- **Match fixing and betting:** throwing or arranging results means a permanent ban and lost prizing. Players, coaches, and staff may not bet on any Emerald Cup match.

- **Behavior:** no harassment, hate speech, threats, or slurs anywhere (Discord, in game, toward opponents or admins). False statements or fake evidence in a ticket are a serious offense. Teams answer for their players, coach, and staff.

- **Demos:** keep them for the whole event and hand them over if asked. Failing to produce requested evidence can be ruled against you.

**9 Penalties**

Admins apply penalties, and they escalate with repeat offenses. Disqualifications are only for offenses in this rulebook and apply to every team equally.

| **Offense**                                                           | **Penalty**                                              |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| **Not ready 20 minutes after default time, or PC still updating**     | Forfeit                                                  |
| **Starting without admin confirmation, or after a rehost without it** | Round results not acknowledged                           |
| **Unapproved stand-in or ineligible player**                          | Match forfeit; possible DQ                               |
| **Failed or refused webcam check**                                    | Map or match forfeit; possible DQ and ban                |
| **Coach left voice channel / coach communicated in live rounds**      | Coaching lost for the match / match forfeit, possible DQ |
| **Pause abuse**                                                       | Warning, then map forfeit                                |
| **Toxicity or harassment**                                            | Warning, suspension, or ban                              |
| **Cheating, circumventing anti-cheat, or match fixing**               | Permanent ban; no prizing                                |

- Repeated forfeits can mean removal with no Team Pass refund. Admins decide how a removed team’s results are handled, up to voiding them.

**10 Disputes, Streaming & Final Notes**

- **Disputes:** open a ticket in the NSTLGA Discord within 24 hours of the match ending (or of the default time for a no-show). Include both team names, the match, what happened, and evidence (screenshots, demos, VODs, timestamps). Don’t DM admins. If it’s urgent, open the ticket and tag Arr0w or a Head Admin inside it. Admins can’t rule on a forfeit, no-show, or technical failure without a ticket. Head Admin decisions are final.

- **Streaming:** NSTLGA holds the broadcast rights. You may stream your own POV with at least a 2-minute delay. Selected matches are broadcast officially, and you may be asked to join a production voice channel. Taking part lets NSTLGA use your team name, logo, handles, and match footage to promote the event.
